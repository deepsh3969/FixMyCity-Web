ABOUT FIXMYCITY

Technology that helps citizens speak up about their city.

FixMyCity is designed as a simple civic communication platform where residents can report problems, view existing complaints and understand the progress of reported issues.

From potholes and garbage to drainage and streetlights, the platform brings everyday civic concerns into one accessible digital space.
